## Main readings

- Braun, V., Clarke, V. "[Using Thematic Analysis in Psychology](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1191/1478088706qp063oa)." *Qualitative Research in Psychology* 2006.
- Roberts, K., Dowell, A., Nie, J.-B. "[Attempting Rigour and Replicability in Thematic Analysis of Qualitative Research Data; a Case Study of Codebook Development](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12874-019-0707-y)." *BMC Medical Research Methodology* 2019.

## More on qualitative methods

- Braun, V., Clarke, V. "[Reflecting on Reflexive Thematic Analysis](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/2159676X.2019.1628806)." *Qualitative Research in Sport, Exercise and Health* 2019.

- Gururaja, S., Bertsch, A., Na, C., Widder, D., Strubell, E. "[To Build Our Future, We Must Know Our Past: Contextualizing Paradigm Shifts in Natural Language Processing](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.822/)." *EMNLP* 2023. [PDF](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.822.pdf)
