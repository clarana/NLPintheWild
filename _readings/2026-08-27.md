## A philosophical roots perspective

- Turing, A. M. "[Computing Machinery and Intelligence](https://doi.org/10.1093/mind/LIX.236.433)." *Mind* LIX(236):433-460, 1950.
- Shannon information theory work, 1950s.

- Zipf's Law, 1932.
- Leibniz and Descartes on "universal language", 1600s: *De Arte Combinatoria*; Alphabet of Human Thought.
