Course Policies

Late Policy

Each student has 5 late days to use throughout the semester for homework assignments. Late days may be used in whole-day increments and at most 3 late days may be used on a single assignment. After late days are exhausted, assignments lose 20% per day. The research project deadlines are firm.

Collaboration & Academic Integrity

Homework assignments must be completed individually unless otherwise specified. You are welcome to discuss high-level ideas with classmates, but your submitted work must be your own. The final project may be completed in groups of up to 2–3 students. You must follow CMU’s Academic Integrity Policy.

Use of Generative AI

You may use generative AI tools (e.g., GitHub Copilot, ChatGPT) for coding tasks in homework assignments, but you must clearly disclose their use and explain the AI-generated portions in your submission. AI tools may not be used for written components (paper responses, project reports) unless explicitly permitted.

Accessibility & Accommodations

If you have a disability and are registered with the Office of Disability Resources, please contact the instructor within the first two weeks of class so we can make appropriate arrangements. We are committed to making this course accessible to all students.

Wellness

Graduate school can be stressful. If you are struggling, please reach out. CMU’s Counseling & Psychological Services (CaPS) is available at 412-268-2922. You can also speak privately with the instructor or any TA.
